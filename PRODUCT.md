## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Astro (static output, zero client JS by default), plain CSS with custom properties, self-hosted fonts, deployed as static files on a CDN (Cloudflare Pages or Vercel). Replaces the WordPress (Divi) site at tachi.id and the earlier Next.js rebuild, which was removed on `feature/rework`.

## Users

Corporate B2B buyers: operations, procurement and marketing people at Indonesian companies (manufacturing, FMCG, energy, freight) who need a dependable logistics partner or a creative agency, and who check a supplier's group company before engaging one of its units.

## Product Purpose

tachi.id is the group landing page for PT Tantra Hachi Group (Tachi Group), a holding company based in Bekasi. It introduces the group, routes visitors to the right business unit, and starts a conversation. Success: a qualified buyer opens a WhatsApp chat with the right unit already named.

## Positioning

One holding, several operating units that share management and refer work to each other: Tachi Logistics (freight, door-to-door, customs, corporate logistics, Sabang to Merauke), Tachi Ads (brand strategy, digital marketing, content, production), Tachi Garage (inspected used cars), and Tachi Foodworks (F&B, in preparation).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Tachi Group / PT Tantra Hachi Group. Slogan: "Stronger Together".
- Logo: infinity loop joined to a downward-pointing samurai sword (protecting, not attacking), in a cyan-to-indigo blue gradient. Files in `brand/`.
- Logo philosophy (client's own words, tachi.id/brand-identity/): Infinity = continuity and connected network; Samurai = protection and responsibility; blue gradient = stability, trust, technological progress; modern typography = strength and future orientation.
- Values: Integrity, Customer Focus, Speed, Collaboration, Innovation.
- Vision: to become a leading, innovative and sustainable national holding group with an integrated distribution network across Indonesia.

## Product Principles

1. Speed is a value the company claims; the site must prove it by loading instantly.
2. Route, don't brochure: every section helps a buyer find the right unit and contact it.
3. Only real proof: client logos and services, never invented numbers or quotes.
4. Indonesian first, English equal.
